### Independent non-cubic unsupported-In2O3 datasets

For a defensible phase-aware calibration, obtain at least one additional
independent study for hexagonal In2O3 and one for rhombohedral In2O3 with:

- temperature, pressure, H2/CO2 ratio, and mass-normalized flow;
- catalyst preparation and phase confirmation;
- CO2 conversion and methanol/CO selectivity on a common basis; and
- preferably tabulated rather than graph-only responses.

This is an evidence request, not a known specific paywalled PDF request.

### Clean In2O3/ZrO2 condition matrix

This remains necessary for the later catalyst bridge, not for Part A. The
desired paper or SI would report a clean, Schuhle/T1-001-comparable
In2O3/ZrO2 catalyst across several temperatures, pressures, feed ratios, or
space velocities with conversion and methanol output. Unsupported-In2O3
calibration cannot replace this evidence.
