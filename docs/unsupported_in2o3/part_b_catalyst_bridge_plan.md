# Part B: In2O3/ZrO2 catalyst-bridge plan

**Status:** next stage after the completed Part A diagnostic

## Purpose

Part B asks whether the clean unsupported-In2O3 work can be connected
scientifically to the In2O3/ZrO2 catalyst used in the existing impurity
evidence. It does not assume that the Ghosh parameters transfer to the
supported catalyst. The support can change phase stability, surface sites,
dispersion, reducibility, adsorption, and reaction branching.

This bridge is required because the final project question concerns the effect
of impurities on methanol output. A defensible impurity calculation needs a
clean reference for the same catalyst family and a clearly defined domain.

## Initial Part B work

1. Inventory every accessible clean In2O3/ZrO2 condition and its source.
2. Preserve phase, In2O3 loading, ZrO2 phase, preparation and pretreatment,
   temperature, pressure, H2:CO2 ratio, flow basis, catalyst mass, time on
   stream, and methanol outcome provenance.
3. Separate directly reported values from calculated and graph-digitized
   values, and keep repeated conditions within their original study.
4. Identify matched or near-matched unsupported/supported conditions without
   treating them as identical experiments.
5. Test whether observed differences can be explained by operating conditions
   alone or require a catalyst/support-specific term.

No parameter fitting is authorized by this plan's audit stage. Any later fit
must use a predeclared structure, keep complete studies together, preserve a
whole-study validation set, and report uncertainty and identifiability.

## Decision gate

Part B will end with one of three evidence-based decisions:

- a bounded, interpretable support correction is supported;
- In2O3/ZrO2 requires its own clean-catalyst model; or
- the current evidence is insufficient for a quantitative bridge, so the
  result remains a structured meta-analysis and gap statement.

Only after that decision will the project attach an H2S or other impurity
response to the appropriate clean-catalyst baseline.

## Boundaries carried forward

- Part A is complete and its parameters/results remain locked.
- Unsupported In2O3, In2O3/ZrO2, and Cu/ZnO/Al2O3 are not interchangeable.
- A successful numerical run is not experimental validation.
- The equation registry must be updated before a new equation affects a
  reported result.
- Source PDFs remain outside the public repository.
